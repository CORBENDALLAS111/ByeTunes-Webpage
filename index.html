FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need a computer every time?

No. After the initial pairing process, you can manage and inject music directly from your iPhone.

Does ByeTunes preserve Apple Music metadata?

Yes. Artwork, lyrics, explicit flags, artist and album data are retained and displayed natively.

Which formats are supported?

MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV and ALAC are supported, including lossless audio workflows.
