Features
Made for people who still care about their library.

Not another downloader interface. ByeTunes focuses on making imported music feel native, organised and clean once it reaches the device.

♪

Original Apple Music metadata

Keep track names, artist data, album artwork and Apple Music-style details instead of dumping anonymous files into a folder.

◉

Animated covers

Support for richer cover presentation so imported tracks feel closer to official Apple Music releases.

⇣

Direct injection

Add songs straight to the iPhone media library after pairing. No repeated iTunes sync workflow.

≋

Queue control

Download albums or selected tracks, check progress, then inject as songs or playlists.

❝

Lyrics support

Imported music can display lyrics in the familiar iOS player experience.

Setup
Pair once. Manage music on-device.

The computer is only needed for the initial pairing file. After that, ByeTunes handles importing directly from the phone.

1
Install LocalDevVPNConnect so ByeTunes can communicate with the required local services.
2
Generate pairing fileCreate the certificate once using idevice tools on your computer.
3
Import into ByeTunesAdd the pairing file, then keep the rest of the workflow on your iPhone.
4
Inject musicAdd songs, albums or playlists directly into the native media library.
