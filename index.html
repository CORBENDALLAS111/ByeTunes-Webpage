FAQ

Clear answers. No filler.

This section makes the site feel like a real product page, not a poster.

Do I need a computer every time?

No. ByeTunes needs pairing setup first, then the day-to-day music management happens on device.

Does it preserve Apple Music metadata?

Yes. ByeTunes focuses on better Apple Music metadata fetching, including artwork, track numbers, album information, lyrics and related fields.

What changed for iOS 26.4?

iOS 26.4 requires RP Pairing for this path. ByeTunes added support for that flow, plus new artwork database and color handling for Apple Music views.

Can I back up my library?

Yes. Recent versions include snapshot backup/restore and full backup options for database and media files.
